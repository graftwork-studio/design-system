Add `AreaChart`. Like `BarChart` and `LineChart`, it has a written summary and a "View as table" toggle showing the same data. Series are stacked. `stacking="percent"` shows each series' share of the total instead, and `curve` sets how points are joined (`smooth`, `linear` or `step`). The chart is focusable, and the arrow keys move its tooltip from point to point.
