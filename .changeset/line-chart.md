Add `LineChart`. Like `BarChart`, it has a written summary and a "View as table" toggle showing the same data. `curve` sets how points are joined (`smooth`, `linear` or `step`) and `points` adds a dot on each data point (`dots`) or a dot and its value (`values`). The chart is focusable, and the arrow keys move its tooltip from point to point. `BarChart` is unchanged.
